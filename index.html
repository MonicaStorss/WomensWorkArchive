Editorial framework

From a lead to a historical record

01 / Community contribution

Visitors nominate people, share memories, and point to materials that may otherwise remain hidden.

02 / Archival research

Project researchers assess evidence of MIT affiliation, historical relevance (late 1960s–2001), and presence in MIT Distinctive Collections.

03 / Attributed record

Only researcher-approved material appears here; entries are separately labeled as community contributions or archival records verified against MIT affiliation, relevant years, and Distinctive Collections holdings.
